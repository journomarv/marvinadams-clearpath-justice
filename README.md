ClearPath Justice

Making legal rights easier to access in practice.

ClearPath Justice is a South African digital justice platform designed to help people navigate criminal record expungement — from understanding eligibility and preparing an application to tracking progress and accessing appropriate legal support.

The current pilot focuses on eligibility screening for cannabis-related criminal convictions, translating complex legal requirements into a simple, guided digital journey.

The Problem

A legal right does not automatically become a practical right.

For many people, criminal record expungement involves navigating complex legislation, administrative requirements, documentation and government processes. Lack of information, uncertainty and administrative friction can prevent eligible people from exercising their rights.

ClearPath Justice is being developed to reduce those barriers.

What ClearPath Does

Assess

A guided digital eligibility assessment helps users understand whether they may qualify for criminal record relief based on applicable legal requirements.

Prepare

The platform is designed to help users understand documentation and prepare the information required for an application.

Manage

Future functionality will allow users to track applications and understand where they are in the process.

Refer

Where human intervention or legal assistance is required, ClearPath is designed to connect users with appropriate support.

Verify

The longer-term vision includes secure, citizen-controlled proof of an expungement outcome without unnecessarily exposing underlying criminal-record information.

Current Pilot

The current ClearPath Justice pilot demonstrates the Assess component through a step-by-step eligibility screening experience.

It is a working web application built with React and Vite and designed with a mobile-first approach.

Roadmap

ClearPath is being developed as infrastructure for accessing legal remedies, rather than as a single-purpose cannabis application.

Planned development includes:

- Expanded criminal record relief pathways
- Application preparation and document support
- Case management and status tracking
- Legal and human-support referrals
- Government and justice-system interoperability
- Secure proof of outcome
- A citizen-controlled Digital Justice Passport
- Assisted-digital access through community organisations and advice offices

Digital Justice Infrastructure

ClearPath is exploring how digital public infrastructure can extend beyond identity, payments and government services to include access to legal remedies.

The underlying principle is:

«Data infrastructure is justice infrastructure.»

The goal is not to replace government decision-making or legal professionals.

AI assists; it does not adjudicate.

ClearPath is intended to help people navigate established legal and administrative processes while keeping decision-making with the appropriate public authorities and legal professionals.

Technology

The current pilot uses:

- React 18
- Vite
- Lucide React
- Responsive, mobile-first design
- Accessibility-focused interface
- Component-based front-end architecture

The platform is designed to evolve into a broader digital justice infrastructure layer.

Android

An Android version is being developed from the existing React/Vite application using Capacitor.

The Android application will provide the same ClearPath Justice experience in an installable mobile format while maintaining the existing web application.

Project Status

Current stage: Working pilot / early validation

Current capability: Cannabis conviction expungement eligibility screening

Development focus: Institutional validation, research, pilot readiness and expansion of the digital justice workflow.

Vision

ClearPath Justice aims to make criminal record relief and other legal remedies easier to understand, navigate and access.

The long-term vision is a trusted digital justice layer that connects citizens to legal remedies, government processes and appropriate human support — while prioritising accessibility, transparency, privacy and citizen control.
